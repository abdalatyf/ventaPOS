# Promo App - Core Business Logic

## Overview
The `Promo` application (`D:\Projects\VentaPOS\Promo`) is a static landing page for marketing the VentaPOS software. There are no backend services, controllers, utilities, or complex frontend scripts managing business state.

## Core Algorithms & Mathematical Steps
- **None**: No calculations, data transformations, or complex algorithms are present in this module.

## State Management Workflows
- **None**: The application is entirely stateless. 

## Fundamental Business Rules
- The application's sole purpose is informational, directing users to download a setup executable (`Venta_Offline_Setup_v1.0.exe`) or contact the sales team via a WhatsApp link API.
- All dynamic behavior is limited strictly to UI presentation logic (e.g., IntersectionObserver for scroll animations).

- Not applicable.
